      Surviving
      
        
      She hunts by sound. Walking is quiet; running, slamming doors and the piano are not. Crouching makes you nearly silent.
      
        
      In the dark she is half blind, but a flashlight in her face gives you away. Turn it off when she is close.
      
        
      If she sees you, break line of sight and hide in a wardrobe. If she watched you climb in, it won't save you.
      
        
      When she spots you she screams first. That second is your head start: sprint, turn a corner, and she is slower than you at full run.
      
        
      Read everything. The house tells you how to leave it. The arrow at the top of the screen always points the way to your next goal, and a small light marks the object when you are close.
